UAV–UGV Mission Replay

Same instance, same road event, two controllers. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Every setting is shown three ways, picked from 40 test instances: the case where replanning helped most, a typical one, and the worst one (where it helps least or hurts). The stress cases cut a road so the map is disconnected, and missions can fail. Averaged over 100 instances on the ring-road map, replanning saves about 7% of energy and 5% of mission time.
